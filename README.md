# Supply Chain Transparency Ledger

A blockchain solution for tracking product shipments and verifying authenticity throughout the supply chain. This system enables suppliers to track their products while providing authenticity confirmation through certified validators.

## Features

- Product shipment tracking with detailed origin information
- Authenticity verification by certified validators
- Supplier shipment portfolio management
- Supply chain transparency and traceability

## Usage

Deploy the contract to start tracking products and verifying authenticity in your supply chain.
